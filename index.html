Community Bulletin

The annual Music Festival returns this summer with over 20 local bands taking the Downtown stage. Tickets are free for residents — just show your community card at the entrance. Volunteers are still needed for the event crew.

Our new Baking Workshop series kicks off next Saturday. Chef Ramya will lead participants through classic South Indian sweets and fusion pastries. Seats are limited to 20 per batch, so register early to avoid disappointment.

The Riverside Yoga Camp runs every Sunday morning from 6 AM to 8 AM along the scenic riverside promenade. All skill levels are welcome. Mats are provided by the council. Bring water and wear comfortable clothes.

Local artists are invited to submit entries for the Community Art Wall project. The council will display selected works in the Town Hall corridor for six months. Submission deadline is the 30th of this month — email your portfolio today.

The Food Fair at Northside Park will celebrate cuisines from 12 different cultures. Stall registrations are open for home chefs and small businesses. Live music and folk dance performances will run throughout the afternoon.

Residents are reminded that the Sports Day registration closes on Friday. Events include sprints, relay races, tug-of-war, and a fun family obstacle course. Prizes are sponsored by local businesses. All ages are welcome to participate.
